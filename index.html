03 · METHODOLOGY

Clear signals.
No false certainty.

FloodFacts is a reference product, not a survey, insurance quote or emergency warning. It joins place identity with public flood evidence and keeps the result’s limits in view.

01

Identity
Addresses and areas are resolved to a stated geography before evidence is shown.

02

Evidence
River, surface-water, coastal and other layers remain distinct signals—not one invented score.

03

Coverage
“Not found”, “not checked”, “not published” and “not applicable” remain separate states.
